# Clubes de pádel del norte de España (País Vasco, Navarra, Cantabria, Asturias, Galicia, La Rioja) como prospectos para una web premium

Research date: 2026-10-15. Main sources: Playtomic club pages (fetched live), Basque Government facility catalogue, Páxinas Galegas, Padelmap, Padelnest, Pistaenjuego, club websites. Note: Playtomic pages rarely show phone, email, Instagram handle or Google rating. Those fields are marked "not found" unless a source published them. No Instagram follower counts or Google ratings could be retrieved (see Gaps).

## Q1. Which real, operating clubs are strong prospects, and what does each one need?

### Takeaway
Most medium indoor clubs in the north have no website of their own. Their only web presence is the Playtomic page, and several of those have no description, contradictory opening hours, or no contact data. The strongest prospects (fit 5) are BPI Zamudio (Bilbao), Pádel Indoor Bidasoa (Irún), Padeleku (Vitoria), La Bandeja (Pamplona), AC Padel Coruña (Cambre) and Padelprix Santiago. All have an escuela and/or competitions but nothing to show them on the web. Every prospect below is 100% indoor. That fits the "rain-proof, all year" angle.

### Inferences
- The common pattern: medium indoor clubs rely 100% on Playtomic, which shows courts and prices but not the story (escuela, coaches, events, gastro, facilities). Several Playtomic pages contain contradictory hours (Bidasoa, La Bandeja), and Arena Pádel's addresses contradict each other. These are concrete, provable hooks for a sales visit.
- Clubs with documented escuela + competitions + no web (BPI, Bidasoa, La Bandeja, AC Padel Coruña, Padelprix) have the most content ready to fill the Clases and El club modules quickly.
- In Basque-speaking areas (Donostia, Irún, Vitoria, Bizkaia) a Basque/Spanish version is a likely expectation and an upsell. A Galician version is optional in A Coruña, Santiago and Vigo.
- Since no phone or email is published for most Playtomic-only clubs, the practical first contact is in person or by Instagram DM.
