# Clubes de pádel en Andalucía, Extremadura y Canarias para vender una web premium

Research date: 2026-10-15. Main sources: Playtomic club pages (fetched), Federación Andaluza de Pádel (fap.es) news, padeladdict.com directories, and the clubs' own websites where I could find and fetch them.

**Important limitation:** the search and fetch tools could not read Instagram, Google Maps ratings or review counts, so **follower counts and Google ratings are missing for every club**. Check them in person or on your phone before visiting. Playtomic pages hide the club's phone, email and website behind the app, so most clubs below have **no published contact found**. That is a lead in itself: if a Playtomic page has no "web" link, the club usually has no website.

## 1. Which clubs are the strongest prospects, and what is wrong with their current web presence?

### Takeaway
The strongest prospects fall into two groups. **(A)** Clubs with a broken, abandoned or placeholder website, which makes for the easiest pitch: Lew Hoad (Mijas), La Capellanía (Alhaurín de la Torre), Los Boliches (Fuengirola) and Padel N Sports (Estepona). **(B)** Medium independent clubs with a school, tournaments and a shop whose only web presence is a Playtomic page: Colors Pádel (Almería), PADELaKademia (Jaén), Campus Pádel Granada, Gran Canaria Padel Indoor (Telde), Padelcross and Iron Padel (Sevilla), Padel Indoor El Ejido, 360 Padel Indoor (La Laguna), Bida (Maspalomas), Bellavista (Huelva) and Nuevo Jacaranda (Jerez).

### Inferences
- Many medium Andalusian and Canarian clubs (Colors, PADELaKademia, Campus Granada, GC Padel Indoor, Padelcross, Iron, El Ejido, 360, Bida) appear to rely **entirely on Playtomic**. Their Playtomic pages often contradict themselves on hours, postcode or court count, which gives you a concrete, easy-to-show pain point when you visit.
- The Costa del Sol clubs listed in the old padeladdict directory are where **dead or neglected domains** show up (La Capellanía hijacked, Los Boliches redirected to Taykus, Padel N Sports empty, Lew Hoad running a template with lorem ipsum). These are the easiest in-person pitches because you can show the problem on your phone.
- English or bilingual sites matter most for Lew Hoad, Los Boliches, Padel N Sports, Bida (Maspalomas) and Green Padel (Arona).
- Good in-person visit routes: **Costa del Sol** (Fuengirola → Mijas → Alhaurín → Estepona), **Almería** (Colors, ProPadel, El Ejido), **Granada** (Campus, Box), **Sevilla** (Iron, Padelcross), **Gran Canaria** (Telde, Maspalomas) and **Tenerife** (La Laguna, Güímar, Arona).
