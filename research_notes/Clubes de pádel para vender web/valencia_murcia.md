# Clubes de pádel en Comunitat Valenciana y Región de Murcia: prospectos para web premium

Research date: 2026-10-15. Sources: Playtomic club pages, club websites (fetched), and directories (Padelnest, PistaEnJuego, Salir.com, Urban Sports Club, Padelwarrior). Search tooling was US-centric. Google Maps and Instagram could not be read directly, so **Instagram follower counts and Google ratings are mostly NOT verified** (see Gaps). Directory data on court counts often conflicts between sources; conflicts are flagged.

## Q1. Which concrete, operating clubs are strong prospects, and why?

### Takeaway
I found 16 real clubs where the web presence is weak or absent. They are mostly mid-sized (5–11 courts), independent, and have an escuela. The strongest are in Murcia city (Nueva Flota, M2, Pádel Zone), where the clubs exist only on Playtomic, and in Cartagena (Padel Center Cartago, which uses a broken IONOS/CM4all builder site). Also strong: Castellón (Play Pádel, on a Matchpoint default page with encoding bugs) and Valencia metro (Dream Padel, a WordPress theme with demo content left in; Desafío Pádel, with no website).

### Cited Findings

**Fit legend:** 5 = ideal (no website or a broken one, mid size, escuela, independent). 3 = possible, but needs a visit to confirm.

### Inferences
- **Best route in Murcia:** an in-person tour of Nueva Flota, M2 and Pádel Zone, all within about 15 km. None has a website, all use Playtomic, and all have an escuela, so the same pitch and demo work for all three.
- **Pitch for Playtomic-only clubs:** Playtomic covers bookings, but it doesn't sell the escuela, the shop or tournaments, and it doesn't rank in Google under the club's own name. The pitch should focus on Clases and Eventos, not on bookings.
- **Pitch for clubs on management-software sites** (Play Pádel on Matchpoint, Cartago on reservadeportes): offer a front-end website that keeps their booking system behind the "Reservar" buttons. That lowers the client's switching fear.
- **Coastal Alicante clubs** (Bela, and Benidorm/Torrevieja generally) have British and Nordic clientele, so offer ES/EN as standard. Murcia and Castellón can stay Spanish-only, apart from Cartagena coast clubs near Mar Menor resorts.
- **A future option for Dream Padel:** it works entirely by WhatsApp, so the WhatsApp-lead forms in the product fit it well. Offering a Playtomic integration later could be an upsell.

## Q2. Which clubs should be avoided (chains, multi-sport giants, or already well served)?

### Takeaway
Skip large multi-sport complexes, gym chains, hotel/resort clubs and the big historic clubs. They have corporate decision chains or existing web teams, and the product is built for independent padel clubs.

### Cited Findings
- **Club de Tenis Valencia** (founded 1905, multi-sport, padel school directed by Vicente Purchal): a historic institution with complex governance. — [Salir.com](https://www.salir.com/donde-jugar-padel-en-valencia-art-3467.html)
- **SUMA Fitness Club Patacona** (Alboraya): a gym chain with other centres in the Valencian Community, where padel is secondary. — same source
- **Sportcity Massanassa**: a large multi-sport complex. — same source
- **La Plana Sport** (Castellón): 14 tennis courts, 9 padel courts, gym, pool and social club, so a multi-sport complex. — [Planomato](https://www.planomato.com/es/castellon-de-la-plana/v/la-plana-sport)
- **Centro Deportivo Asprillas** (Elche): 13 courts, more than 250 students, school since 2008, own website (cdasprillas.es). Bigger and already online. — [cdasprillas.es](https://cdasprillas.es/); [cda1970.es](https://www.cda1970.es/escuelas-padel/)
- **PadelPoint at Meliá Villaitana** (Benidorm): a hotel or resort operation. — [The Padel Paper](https://thepadelpaper.com/where-play-padel-holiday-spain/)
- **Club MMGR, Mar Menor Golf Resort** (near Cartagena): a resort club offering RPP certification courses and its own booking office. — [Murcia Today](https://murciatoday.com/padel_master_classes_and_match_lessons_at_club_mmgr_mar_menor_golf_resort_2337047-a.html)
- **Club de Tenis Los Álamos** (Lorca): a multi-sport tennis club with its own website. It has 6 padel courts approved by the FPRM, 2 of them covered. — [clubdeportivolosalamos.com](https://clubdeportivolosalamos.com/padel/)
- **OkPadel Centro Deportivo** (Castellón): up to 10 covered panoramic courts, one of the largest indoor complexes in the city, with mixed management reviews. A low priority until its website is checked. — [padelgo.es](https://padelgo.es/padel/padel-castellon-okpadel-centro-deportivo/)

### Inferences
- The Avoid list is based on size and structure. I did not confirm that their websites are good, except for Asprillas and Los Álamos, which do have their own websites.

### Gaps
- Big chains (Padel Nuestro, Ocean Padel, Indoor Padel Center franchises, etc.) were not specifically searched in these regions. Any club that turns out to be a franchise location should be skipped.
