# Clubes de pádel prospectos (Cataluña, Aragón, Islas Baleares) para vender web premium

Research date: 2026-10-15. Method: web search plus Playtomic club pages and club websites where found. Limits: the search tool was rate-limited in places (one Zaragoza query failed). Instagram follower counts and Google ratings could not be retrieved (Instagram/Google Maps are not readable through these tools), so they are listed as gaps. "No own web found" means no club website turned up in search and none is linked on Playtomic. **Before visiting, check each club's Google Maps profile to confirm there is no website.**

## Which real, operating medium-sized independent clubs are strong prospects, and what does each need?

### Takeaway
I found 10 strong or medium prospects across the three regions. Most are 4–11-court clubs that run escuela, torneos/americanas, tienda and cafetería, but have **no website found at all**: their only online presence is the Playtomic page. The best fits are Pádel Club Mallorca (Palma), Padel Basic Factory (Sabadell), Padel Indoor Aragón (Zaragoza), Regal Padel Club (Zaragoza), Pàdel Indoor Figueres (Girona) and Punto Pádel (Santa Oliva, Tarragona). Coverage of the Barcelona metro area is thin. It needs a second pass.

### Inferences
- Playtomic is the booking platform for every prospect found. The "Reservar" module can be a simple deep link to each club's Playtomic page, so no integration work is needed.
- The common pattern is a medium club that already has the content (escuela, prices, torneos, tienda) inside Playtomic but no own domain. The pitch is "Google visibility plus WhatsApp class leads", not "replace your booking system".
- Catalonia (Girona especially) needs a Catalan version, and Mallorca clubs benefit from English (and maybe German). Price these as add-ons.
- Best route for an in-person tour: Zaragoza cluster (Indoor Aragón, Regal, Cuarte, Utebo) in one day; Girona cluster (Figueres, Vilablareix, UALA) in one day; Palma (Pádel Club Mallorca, Pins) in one trip.
- The Pàdel Girona UALA privacy policy issue (copied Guardian text) is a concrete, verifiable hook for an email pitch.

### Gaps
- Instagram handles and follower counts (except @padelgironauala) and Google ratings/review counts could not be retrieved. Check them on Instagram/Google Maps before contact.
- For clubs 1–9, I found no business phone, email or WhatsApp published by the club in the sources. Take them from Google Maps or the club in person. I did not use third-party contact data.
- "No web" is based on search results and Playtomic pages. A club may still have a site linked only from Google Maps or Instagram, so verify.
- The Barcelona city and metro area (beyond Sabadell) is under-covered because of search rate limits. Candidates to research next: independent clubs in Badalona, L'Hospitalet, Mataró, Granollers, Sant Cugat and Castelldefels. Tarragona city and Lleida city also need more independent prospects.
- Court counts for Utebo and the indoor/outdoor split for Vilablareix were not confirmed. The Aspanoa tournament year is unknown.
- Playtomic pages are cached snapshots, so prices and hours may be outdated.
