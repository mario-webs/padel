# Clubes de pádel en la Comunidad de Madrid: prospectos para vender una web premium

Research date: 2026-10-15. All sites were fetched on that date with an automated fetcher (it reads the HTML, not a rendered mobile view). Mobile rendering, page speed, Instagram follower counts and most Google review counts were **not** verified; see Gaps. Ratings shown come from the padeldir.com directory (a Google Maps mirror), not from Google directly.

## Which Madrid clubs have strong activity (classes, ligas, many reviews) but a poor or missing website?

### Takeaway
The south industrial belt (Getafe polígono Los Olivos / calle Morse, Móstoles) has a cluster of independent indoor clubs, 4–16 courts each, all with escuela and competitions, running neglected WordPress or template sites. MG Indoor (spam injected into its site), Pádel Sport Indoor Getafe (© 2020, no contact data) and Novo Pádel (raw shortcode and developer text visible) are the strongest pitches. A second group has **no website at all**, only a Playtomic page: SetPoint Getafe, Padel Indoor Alcalá, VIM Padel Madrid, Rivapadel (13 courts, 650+ students), Madriz PadelSur, Padel Padre Damián and Padel Para Todos.

### Inferences
- Getafe's Los Olivos / calle Morse area has 4–5 indoor clubs within walking distance of each other: MG Indoor, PSIG, SetPoint, Madriz PadelSur and BamVolea Get Indoor. That makes it the most efficient day of in-person visits. Móstoles (Novo, Pádel 2.0, Coimbra) is a second cluster.
- "No website" clubs (SetPoint, Padel Indoor Alcalá, Rivapadel, VIM, Padre Damián, Padel Para Todos) are easier to pitch than clubs that already paid an agency (JMO, Buenavista), but they publish no email or phone on Playtomic, so they need an in-person visit or Instagram DMs.
- MG Indoor's spam injection is a security problem, not just a style one. It is the most urgent and concrete opener, and it would be worth screenshotting before the visit.
- BamVolea appears to be absorbing independent Madrid clubs (Get Indoor, Ciudad de la Raqueta, "BamVolea Punta Chica" and "BamVolea Padel Training" in Alcalá and Rivas). Check for the BamVolea brand before approaching any club.

### Gaps
- Instagram follower counts were not checked for any club, because Instagram pages need a login.
- Google review counts were not obtained. The ratings come from padeldir's mirror of Google Maps, with no counts.
- Mobile rendering and page speed were not measured (the fetcher reads HTML only). Run PageSpeed Insights on mgpadel.com, padelsportindoorgetafe.com, novopadel.com and padeldospuntocero.es before visiting.
- Phone and email are missing for SetPoint, Padel Indoor Alcalá, Rivapadel, VIM, Padre Damián and Padel Para Todos. Playtomic does not publish them, and the directory numbers are not club-published.
- Court counts are unknown for PSIG and Coimbra.
- Independence and ownership are unverified for all prospects.
- The madridcentralpadel.com SSL error may be specific to the fetcher.
- Majadahonda, Pozuelo, Boadilla, Torrejón, Arganda and Madrid's southern districts were not covered (Torrejón's Pádel Soto, Padel Solana and RR Padel, and Arganda's Funky Padel, were seen in search results but not researched) — [Playtomic search](https://playtomic.com/es/clubs/padel-indoor-alcala), [Playtomic Rivapadel](https://playtomic.com/clubs/rivapadel-sport-club)

## What booking platform do they use?

### Takeaway
Playtomic dominates: 12 of the 14 prospects use it, either as a plain link or a white-label page. The exceptions are MG Indoor (padelclick plus Playtomic) and Novo Pádel (its own /reservas page, platform not identified). Club Buenavista, on the avoid list, uses its own booking module. The Pistas module should therefore default to a "Reservar en Playtomic" deep link, with the platform configurable.
